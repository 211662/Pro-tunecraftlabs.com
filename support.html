Support

We are here to help you get the most from TuneCraft Labs apps.

How do I get help with an app?+

Email support@tunecraftlabs.com with the app name, device model, Android version, and a short description of the issue.

Why does a tuner need microphone access?+

Tuner apps use your device microphone to detect instrument pitch. Review the applicable app privacy policy for full details.

How do I restore a purchase?+

Look for Restore Purchases in Settings or the purchase screen. Contact support if you still need help.

How do I report a bug or send feedback?+

Email support with the app name, device, Android version, and reproduction steps.

How can I request deletion of my data?+

Visit our Account & Data Deletion page or email privacy@tunecraftlabs.com.
